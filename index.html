How to Play

    Blast the crates to find the hidden key, defeat every creature, then unlock the exit. Power-ups reset every stage, and so do your lives (back to 3), but each stage hides a few in its crates. Careful: blasts destroy power-ups and skulls lying on the floor. A skull is a curse: slow legs, endless bombs or long fuses for a few seconds. Flames stay deadly for a moment, so wait for the embers.

    Move: arrows / WASD or the pad
    Bomb: Space or the Bomb button
    Detonate: E or Boom
    Menu: Esc or P

    Collect coins from crates and defeated creatures, then spend them in the Shop on new characters, hats and accessories. Pick D-pad or Joystick in Settings.
